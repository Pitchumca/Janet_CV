Executive Profile

Higher education leader with 29+ years of experience in university governance, institutional transformation, research and innovation, accreditation and rankings, and industry-integrated education. Currently Principal of AVIT, Chennai; previously Pro Vice Chancellor of Sanjivani University and, for over seven years, Principal of Sri Krishna College of Engineering & Technology (Autonomous), Coimbatore, which entered the NIRF Top-100 in four consecutive years (2020–2023) and earned NAAC 'A' Grade and NBA accreditation for seven departments.

Brings a record of measurable execution: ₹9.83 crore in institutional research grants mobilised, 11 Ph.D. scholars guided, 24 patents, 59+ active industry partnerships, and industry-powered degree programmes with TCS and Virtusa. Leadership approach is aligned with NEP 2020, outcome-based education, AI and emerging technologies, entrepreneurship, sustainability and inclusive higher education.
