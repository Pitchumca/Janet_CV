Executive Profile

Strategic vision, measurable execution

Higher education leader with 29+ years in university governance, institutional transformation, research and accreditation. Currently Principal of AVIT, Chennai, and formerly Pro Vice Chancellor of Sanjivani University. As Principal of Sri Krishna College of Engineering & Technology (Autonomous), Coimbatore, for more than seven years, she led the institution into the NIRF Top-100 for four consecutive years, secured NAAC 'A' Grade and NBA accreditation for seven departments, and mobilised ₹9.83 crore in research funding.

Her leadership builds industry-integrated, AI-enabled institutions aligned with NEP 2020 and outcome-based education, with 59+ active industry partnerships, 24 patents and 11 doctoral scholars guided.
